# Choosing between automatic and manual withdrawals

FlowPayroll describes two ways to manage withdrawals. The right choice depends on whether you prefer scheduled transfers or direct control over each request.

## Automatic withdrawals

In **Automatic** mode, eligible funds can be transferred to your designated primary wallet according to the configured schedule, without requiring a new request each time.

This may suit people who prefer a regular payout routine or do not want to initiate every withdrawal themselves.

Before enabling this option, set up the **primary payout wallet**, confirm its address and network, and check the applicable schedule. Each scheduled withdrawal remains subject to review and processing requirements.

## Manual withdrawals

With **Manual** mode, you decide when to submit a request and how much of the available balance to transfer.

This can be useful when you want to combine several incoming payments, choose particular payout dates, or evaluate conversion conditions before withdrawing. Requests are still reviewed before release, and completion times can vary.

## Quick comparison

| Your priority | Suggested mode |
| --- | --- |
| A recurring withdrawal schedule | **Automatic** |
| Choosing the exact amount and timing | **Manual** |
| Combining funds into fewer transfers | **Manual** |
| Reducing routine withdrawal steps | **Automatic** |

## Before you choose

Confirm that the saved wallet supports the selected USDT network, review any applicable fees or limits, and verify whether automatic withdrawal settings are available to your account. Avoid assuming that either mode guarantees immediate settlement.
