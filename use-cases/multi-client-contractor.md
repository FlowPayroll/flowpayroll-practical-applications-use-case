# Managing payments from multiple contracting clients

**Example user:** An independent consultant or small agency receiving payments of different sizes from several customers throughout the month.

**The challenge:** It can be difficult to reconcile incoming transfers when invoices, payment confirmations, and banking records are scattered across different systems.

## Example workflow

1. **Create a separate company record** in FlowPayroll for each paying client.
2. **Prepare a funding request per payment** in USD or EUR, using the appropriate amount and client details.
3. **Follow each request's progress** from **Pending** to **Paid** as transfers arrive. Successfully received payments contribute to the available balance.
4. **Select a withdrawal approach.** Subject to availability, configure **Automatic** payouts to a primary wallet or request specific amounts through **Manual** withdrawals. Eligible bank or USDT withdrawal options may be offered.

## Why this organization can help

- **Consolidated visibility:** Review the combined amount collected while keeping separate records for individual clients.
- **Easier reconciliation:** Identify which requests are complete and which still need follow-up.
- **Flexible withdrawal planning:** Decide between a predefined schedule and on-demand requests.

## Recordkeeping tips

- Use consistent client names and meaningful references on funding requests.
- Reconcile every paid request against the original invoice and transfer confirmation.
- Keep invoices, transaction references, and receipts organized for accounting and tax purposes.
- Compare payout methods in the [automatic versus manual guide](automatic-vs-manual.md).
