# International payments for independent freelancers

**Example user:** A designer, developer, copywriter, or marketing consultant working with clients in other countries.

**The challenge:** Receiving overseas payments may involve bank transfer delays, intermediary charges, and different invoicing or payout tools. A freelancer may want to track the original client payment separately from the method used to access those funds.

## Example workflow

1. **Create a company record** in FlowPayroll for each client who will send payment.
2. **Issue a funding request** for the agreed USD or EUR amount and provide the client with the corresponding payment instructions.
3. **Monitor the status.** The request appears as **Pending** while awaiting funds and changes to **Paid** once the transfer has been received and recorded.
4. **Choose a payout destination.** When eligible funds are available, request withdrawal to a supported local bank account or USDT wallet on TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20.
5. **Allow for review and processing.** The request is checked before release; its final timing depends on the relevant providers and destination.

## Potential advantages

- **Separate collection from withdrawal:** Receive client funds first and make the payout decision afterward.
- **Clear blockchain selection:** Choose the intended USDT network instead of relying on an unspecified wallet destination.
- **Additional verification:** Pre-release review may help surface mistakes, but does not replace checking payment details yourself.

## Practical reminders

- Keep an invoice for every engagement and match it to the related payment record.
- For USDT withdrawals, confirm that both the sending service and receiving wallet support the exact same blockchain. Network costs and compatibility vary.
- Retain funding confirmations, withdrawal receipts, and other records needed for bookkeeping and tax reporting.
