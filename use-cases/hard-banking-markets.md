# Getting paid where international banking access is limited

**Example user:** A remote professional in a location where receiving foreign-currency bank transfers can be slow, costly, or subject to restrictions.

**The challenge:** Even when clients can initiate payments, intermediary banking arrangements, local rules, and account limitations may affect access to the funds.

## Example workflow

1. **Collect eligible client payments** through a FlowPayroll funding request in USD or EUR, subject to supported countries and account requirements.
2. **Consider a USDT withdrawal** on a supported network (TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20), if available and legally permitted in your jurisdiction.
3. **Choose how to access local currency.** Where permitted, a regulated exchange or another compliant conversion provider may be used. Conversion options, rates, liquidity, and fees differ by location.
4. **Alternatively, use a bank withdrawal** if your local account and destination are supported.

## What to evaluate

- **Transfer pathways:** A blockchain transfer may reduce some banking dependencies, but it does not eliminate service-provider reviews, network congestion, or off-ramp delays.
- **Value and currency risk:** USDT aims to track the US dollar but can trade away from its target and introduces issuer, custody, and redemption risks.
- **Choice of payout destination:** Available bank and crypto methods depend on local law, supported destinations, and account eligibility.

## Important considerations

- Check your jurisdiction's regulations, reporting requirements, and the terms of each payment provider before proceeding.
- Compare total costs, including platform, blockchain, exchange, and conversion fees rather than assuming one network will always be cheaper.
- Record invoices, dates, amounts, exchange rates, transaction identifiers, and payouts for your records.
