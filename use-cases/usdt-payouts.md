# Planning safer USDT withdrawals

**Example user:** A freelancer or contractor who wants to receive eligible earnings in USDT rather than withdrawing exclusively through a bank.

**The challenge:** Cryptocurrency transfers may be difficult or impossible to reverse. Sending funds to an incorrect address or unsupported blockchain can result in permanent loss.

## Example workflow

1. **Register a destination wallet.** Save the USDT address together with its supported network: TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20. Where supported, designate a primary wallet and retain another destination for specific withdrawals.
2. **Receive client payments** through USD or EUR funding requests and verify that the corresponding funds have been credited.
3. **Create a USDT withdrawal request.** Check the destination address, token, network, and amount carefully before submitting.
4. **Complete the review stage.** FlowPayroll describes a pre-release review process. Processing and on-chain confirmation times may vary, and review cannot guarantee recovery from user or provider mistakes.

## Benefits of a structured process

- **Explicit network selection:** Labeling the blockchain helps avoid ambiguity when copying wallet addresses.
- **Cost visibility:** Different networks may have different transaction costs, exchange support, and withdrawal requirements.
- **An additional checkpoint:** Reviewing payout details before transfer can reduce the likelihood of some errors without eliminating risk.

## Safety checklist

- Check the full receiving address using an independent trusted source; do not rely only on its first and last characters.
- Ensure the destination accepts **USDT on the exact network selected**. Never assume that identical-looking addresses mean networks are interchangeable.
- For unfamiliar destinations, consider a small test transfer if fees, limits, and service policies permit.
- Verify the current fee schedule and keep transaction hashes and confirmation records.
