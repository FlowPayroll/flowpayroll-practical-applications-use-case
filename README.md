# FlowPayroll Payment Use Cases

> Practical examples of how freelancers and independent contractors can manage international client payments with **[FlowPayroll](https://flowpayroll.net)**. The workflows cover collecting USD or EUR payments and requesting withdrawals to a local bank or a USDT wallet on supported networks, subject to account eligibility and review.

This guide walks through common payment scenarios, from tracking incoming transfers to choosing a withdrawal method. It is intended to help readers understand the process and evaluate which approach fits their needs.

**Topics:** international freelancer payments · contractor income · stablecoin withdrawals · invoicing · remote work · cross-border transfers

## The FlowPayroll workflow

1. **Register a paying company.** Add the business or client from which you expect to receive funds.
2. **Prepare a funding request.** Specify the amount in USD or EUR and share the applicable transfer instructions. The request shows **Pending** until the incoming payment is received and recorded as **Paid**.
3. **Track available funds.** Completed funding requests increase your account balance; withdrawals reduce the amount available.
4. **Select a withdrawal method.** Request a transfer to an eligible local bank account or a USDT address on a supported network (TRC20, ERC20, or BEP20). Where available, choose between scheduled **Automatic** withdrawals and individually initiated **Manual** withdrawals.
5. **Complete the review process.** Withdrawal requests undergo checks before release. Processing time depends on verification, provider availability, network conditions, and the destination.

## Features to consider

- **Bank and stablecoin destinations:** Select an eligible payout method based on your circumstances and the options currently supported.
- **Visible network selection:** Identify the intended USDT blockchain before submitting a transfer.
- **Pre-release review:** Withdrawal checks offer an additional opportunity to detect issues; they cannot guarantee that every error will be caught.
- **Transparent terms:** Confirm current charges, conversion costs, network fees, withdrawal limits, and processing times before initiating a transaction.
